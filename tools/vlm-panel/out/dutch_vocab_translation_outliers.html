Two items worth human review

  • puddle / "de plas" — perfect in English; in Dutch the panel picks the bubble picture nearly as often. The translation is correct, but it's a weaker cue against that distractor image.
  • dumpling / "de knoedel" — splits evenly with meatball. A knoedel and a meatball are near-identical round shapes; the word doesn't disambiguate the too-close distractor.

The rest are either genuinely hard low-frequency words (mammalogy) or small drops within noise. None are mistranslations — the Dutch words are correct; these are cases where the translated word + distractor-image pairing discriminates worse than English. Still a legitimate pre-launch flag for the item-bank team.
